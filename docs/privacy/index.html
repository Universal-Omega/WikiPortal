WikiPortal

A Universal MediaWiki Client

Alpha Release

One app for every wiki

WikiPortal is a free, open-source client for MediaWiki-powered wikis. Add Wikipedia, Wiktionary, any Miraheze-hosted community wiki, or your own self-hosted wiki by URL, and browse them all from one app, with a proper native reading experience instead of a bookmarked browser tab.

Features

Add any wiki

Point it at any MediaWiki site's URL and it's ready to browse, with its own skin and favicon.

Read offline

Save pages for offline reading, so a page you found is still there without a connection.

Tabs and history

Keep multiple pages open at once, and pick up your recent reading history, all kept on your device.

Explore what's trending

See what's currently popular on each wiki you follow, pulled from that wiki's own public page-view statistics.

Search across your wikis

Find articles by title or content without leaving the app.

No ads, no tracking

No analytics SDK, no ad network, no account required, and no server of ours in between you and the wikis you read.

Your data stays yours

WikiPortal has no backend server. It talks directly to whichever wiki you're browsing, the same way any browser would, and keeps everything else, your added wikis, saved pages, and history, only on your own device. Read the full Privacy Policy for details.
